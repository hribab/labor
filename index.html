Labor

An autonomous product builder that turns an idea into working software and brings it to production.

Labor generates a complete first version of your product, deploys it into the Google Cloud project you choose, prepares the public release, and helps manage features, analytics, and product growth after launch.

Why Labor requests Google access

Google Sign-In provides your basic account identity so Labor can protect and open the correct workspace. Only when you separately choose to connect Google Cloud, Labor requests permission to list projects you can access and to configure Firebase and Google Cloud resources in the project you select. Labor uses this access only to perform the product-building, deployment, release, and management workflows you request.
