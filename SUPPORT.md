# Labor Support

Labor is maintained in public.

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hribab/labor/discussions)
- **Reproducible bugs:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hribab/labor/issues)
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue
- **Privacy requests:** [hari@onroad.app](mailto:hari@onroad.app)

Before asking for help, search existing discussions and issues. Include the smallest reproducible description, the relevant workflow stage, browser/runtime versions, and redacted logs.

Community support is best effort. The project does not currently offer a paid support tier, in keeping with the Forever Free Pledge.
