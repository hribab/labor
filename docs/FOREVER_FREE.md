# The Labor Forever Free Pledge

Labor will never have a subscription, premium tier, or paid feature gates.

Every capability maintained as part of the official Labor project will remain available in the public repository. A feature will not be held back from self-hosters to create a paid edition. The hosted Labor interface will not charge a Labor fee or add a markup to a builder's model or cloud usage.

## Why

Hardworking solopreneurs should not spend sleepless nights stitching together tools that should work for them.

Great entrepreneurs should not be stopped because a builder is missing from the room.

New graduates should be able to turn an idea into something useful without capital, a large team, or gatekeepers.

Building is becoming a form of economic participation. The tools that turn an idea into a real product should expand who can participate, not create another gate.

## What the pledge means

- No Labor subscription.
- No premium Labor plan.
- No feature reserved for paying Labor users.
- No closed-source official edition with capabilities withheld from this repository.
- No markup on model or cloud usage.
- Public discussion before any governance change that could weaken this pledge.

## What can still cost money

Labor brings your model and your cloud. OpenAI, Google Cloud, domain registrars, and other providers may charge for tokens, builds, storage, hosting, bandwidth, domains, or related services. Those providers bill the accounts you connect under their own terms.

Labor cannot promise that third-party infrastructure will be free. It promises that Labor will not become the toll booth.

## Open source and commercial use

Labor is licensed under AGPL-3.0. Open-source licenses permit commercial use. A person or company may operate Labor, sell support, or charge for a service, subject to the license.

The Forever Free Pledge is a commitment about the official Labor project and the service maintained by its stewards. It is not an extra license restriction and does not reduce the freedoms granted by AGPL-3.0.

## Accountability

If a proposed change appears to create a paid gate, hide an official capability, or move community work into a closed edition, raise it in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hribab/labor/discussions). The project should answer that concern in public.

This pledge belongs in the open because promises are stronger when the community can point to them.
