# Labor Community Code of Conduct

Labor exists to help more people build. The community should make that possibility feel real.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and curious.
- Critique ideas and code without diminishing the person behind them.
- Make room for new builders and explain context without condescension.
- Share evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and responsible disclosure.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Pressuring someone to reveal customer data, private source, or security details publicly.
- Abusing community spaces for spam, fraud, or manipulation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hari@onroad.app](mailto:hari@onroad.app). Include relevant links or screenshots. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the people involved.

This code applies in Labor-managed repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review spaces, and project events.
